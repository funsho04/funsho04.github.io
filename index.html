Cost Variance Analysis (Excel) ↗

Ambike Financial Inc. recorded a consolidated FY-2026 actual expenditure of $702,680 against a budget of $688,050, resulting in an unfavorable net variance of -$14,630 (-2.1%). Core operational categories across Sales, Marketing, Operations, and G&A maintained exceptional discipline with a combined variance of only -$500.

Click for moreShow less

The organizational variance is overwhelmingly driven by R&D and AI cost overruns (-$14,600 and -$14,130, respectively). Confirmation from the R&D President verifies this is entirely attributable to Project Catalyst—an unbudgeted client initiative onboarding 6 new engineers. Crucially, baseline R&D operations achieved -$1,500 in operational savings, proving the variance stems strictly from unbudgeted project scale.

Onyx Financial Analysis (Power BI) ↗

The Onyx Financial Analysis dashboard shows 2023 revenue of $17.6M and net profit of $4.3M, with a 61.78% gross profit margin and a 29.86% EBIT margin ($5.2M).

Click for moreShow less

Sports equipment ($2.3M net income, 25.84% margin) and sportswear ($2.7M, 40.2% margin) drive most of the profit. The nutrition and food supplements line is loss-making: $1.8M of revenue against $1.012M of COGS, a net loss of $712.88K and a -38.67% margin. Operating expenses of $5.6M take 33.71% of revenue, and interest and taxes of $929K consume nearly 18% of EBIT. The analysis recommends fixing or exiting the nutrition line, optimizing operating costs, and reviewing debt and tax strategy.
